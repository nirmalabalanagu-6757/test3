Generate or record each clip separately, then join them in order with straight cuts. The final video lasts four minutes when every clip is exactly 10 seconds.

PRESENTATION NOTES
- This explains the proposed future operating model in the image, not verified current platform behavior.
- CRE routes requests to appropriate teams and coordinates help; it does not broadcast every request to every team or perform every specialist task.
- Use only the agents relevant to a particular request. All 15 remain visible in the image.
- The ITIL Change Process Agent supports change preparation and required approvals; it does not replace the accountable approval authority.
- An approved action or tool response still needs service validation. Recovery follows an approved procedure when applicable and is not guaranteed to succeed.
- Only close the request after the agreed outcome is verified; unresolved work retains a responsible owner.
- Do not reintroduce the removed Unified Cloud Foundation, standalone CRE Agent role box, Human Governance checklist, cloud descriptions, or operating-model bullets.
