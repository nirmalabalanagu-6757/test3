RECOMMENDED STORY
1. State the DIC Vision 2030 mission and whom it serves.
2. Explain what the proposed vision would mean to end users.
3. Acknowledge today's manual service delivery and introduce the proposed agentic AI architecture.
4. Follow one business request through clarification, CRE coordination, planning, approval, and controlled execution.

6. Close with continuing support, improvement, and the intended benefits.

CORE MESSAGE
Our DIC Vision 2030 aims to provide cloud services to all PSLs and Airbus Commercial. The proposed agentic AI architecture would coordinate service delivery and automate approved work, with accountable teams and human oversight.
The intended end-user experience is easier requests, coordinated delivery, clear progress, verified outcomes, and continuing support.

CONTINUOUS READ-THROUGH
DIC MISSION, END-USER VALUE, AND THE PROPOSED 2030 CHANGE
Our DIC Vision 2030 aims to provide cloud services to all PSLs and Airbus Commercial. For end users, this would mean easier service requests, coordinated delivery, clear ownership, and support after the service goes live. Today, our teams manually coordinate requirements, approvals, implementation, validation, and handover across the service delivery process. Our proposed agentic AI architecture would coordinate this work, automate approved routine tasks, and keep people responsible for decisions and exceptions.

WHAT THE END USER REQUESTS AND HOW CRE COORDINATES
Imagine you need a new application environment, more storage, or a database. You would begin by describing the business requirement. For our example, a manufacturing team would request an application environment with computing capacity, a database, storage, backup, and monitoring. Agents would clarify capacity, availability, delivery expectations, and ownership, confirming missing details with the requester before preparing the plan. CRE would bring together the appropriate specialists and teams, sharing the same request information so everyone works toward the agreed outcome.

ONE DELIVERY PLAN AND THE REQUIRED APPROVALS
Cloud, Compute, database, and storage specialists would prepare one delivery plan, covering the environment, required resources, dependencies, and task order. Cyber Security, Backup, and FinOps would contribute security controls, protection requirements, and cost considerations before the plan moves forward. The ITIL Change Process Agent would help prepare the change details, validation checks, and recovery plan for the required review. The responsible people would review the change, confirm the permitted scope, and authorize work within the agreed change window.

CONTROLLED EXECUTION AND A CLEAR USER EXPERIENCE
Once authorized, agents would use approved tools and runbooks to create resources and configure the service in the correct sequence. The workflow would track progress, check policies, manage conflicting actions, and pause affected tasks when an issue needs investigation. Security, backup, and required scheduled jobs would be configured, with monitoring connected to support the service after handover. You would receive progress updates and know the responsible team, helping you understand the request's status and any required actions.

VALIDATED SERVICE HANDOVER AND OWNERSHIP
Before handover, agents would validate connectivity, capacity, security, and backup readiness against the checks agreed with the service owner. You would receive a validated environment, access details, support contacts, and delivery evidence, with clear ownership for continuing support. CMDB would be updated with the delivered configuration, dependencies, and ownership, keeping service information available for future support. The request would close after the agreed service outcome is confirmed, with support arrangements ready for the business to use.

CONTINUING SUPPORT AND IMPROVEMENT
After delivery, agents would assess alerts and requests, check the actual service state, and perform approved recovery where appropriate. CRE would coordinate additional help and route unresolved issues to the responsible incident owner, keeping ownership clear throughout recovery. We would use availability, change success, recovery time, and request completion measures to improve runbooks and future service delivery. Our aim is simple: easier requests, faster delivery, and reliable support for all PSLs and Airbus Commercial.
